How the figures were preparedكيف أُعدت الأرقام

Built automatically from the system exports in the Google Drive folder (Profit and Loss-2026-01, Profit and Loss-2026-2, Profit and Loss-2026-3, Profit and Loss-2026-4, Profit and Loss-2026-5, Profit and Loss-2026-6, Profit and Loss-2026-07-.xls, Profit and Loss-2026-8.xls, Profit and Loss-2026-9.xls, Profit and Loss-2026-10), current month and YTD columns. Norms are typical ranges for a full-service resort, not this hotel's budget. Currency assumed to be Egyptian pounds.
